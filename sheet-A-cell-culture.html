Set up

  • Book: hood and Incucyte on PPMS. Everything else on the Carragher portal**.
  • Review the cells under the microscope. Estimate confluence, note appearance.
  • Decide the split ratio or seeding plan before you start.
  • Take media and reagents out to warm (see reagent table).
  • Check tips, stripette bin, aspirator trap, ethanol bottle.

Cleaning

  • Chemgene [optional] — a broad-spectrum disinfectant that kills bacteria, fungi, viruses and mycoplasma. Spray and wipe before** ethanol. Ethanol alone does not clear mycoplasma.
  • 70 % ethanol — all hood surfaces, and every item** going in.
  • Wipe down again at the end. Sash down, hood off.

Fume hood

  • Green on — the sash rises automatically.
  • Red silences the alarm. Yellow is the light.
  • Let it run a few minutes before working.
  • Off**: press sash-down, which powers it off.

Centrifuge

  • Switch on, open the lid. Check all buckets are present, seated, clean and dry.
  • Load balanced, with caps securely closed. Always use lids for buckets.
  • Choose settings, start.
  • The spin should be almost silent. Any rumble — stop and rebalance.
  • Once it ends and alarms, open, remove every tube, leave open and off.
Settings vary by application. They are given with each procedure.

MEDIA REFRESH

Take into the hood
Pipette boy25 mL stripettesWarmed media
  • Look at the cells. Record appearance and confluence.
  • Aspirate all but a small amount of media, from a corner, never touching the cell floor.
  • Add ~15 mL of the appropriate warmed media.
  • Label the T75 with the refresh date. Return to the incubator.

SEEDING PLATES

Take into the hood
Pipette boyStripettesP200 / P1000 + tipsUniversals for each mixLabelled platesWarmed media
Volumes: [see Seeding calculator](https://j-cafferkey.github.io/seeding-calculator.html)
  • Master seed = the resuspended pellet in a universal. Plate seed** = that, diluted to the density you want, with wiggle room.
  • Write the sums in the lab book and keep it by the hood.
  • Label each universal: top = line + PLATE SEED; side = line, x in y.
  • In the hood, add the media first, then the master seed.
  • Volumes under 1 mL use a micropipette, not a stripette.
  • Resuspend the master seed immediately before drawing from it.
  • Aliquot onto the plate, shoogle on two axes, incubate.

Split ratio

1:1 returns every recovered cell to the new flask, which overgrows. 1:2 is half, 1:10 a tenth. The two levers are the volume the pellet is resuspended in and the concentration of that seed.

Reading the media

ColourMeansAction
Pink / salmonnormal, pH ~7.4none
Yellowacidic — overgrown, or bacterialcheck confluence; if turbid, bin
Purple / deep pinkalkaline — CO₂ low, or lid loosecheck the incubator and the cap
Turbid overnightbacterialbin the flask, do not treat
Fine motile specksbacterial, earlybin the flask
Fluffy strands or spheresfungalbin the flask, check the incubator
Clear, but slow and grainy cellsmycoplasmaquarantine, test

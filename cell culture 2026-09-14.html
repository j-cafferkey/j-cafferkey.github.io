Split ratio

1:1 would return every recovered cell to the new flask, which overgrows. 1:2 is half, 1:10 a tenth. The two levers are the volume the pellet is resuspended in and the concentration of that seed.

Centrifuge_ model, S2.11

  • On, lid open. Check all buckets present, seated, clean and dry.
  • Load. Caps tight, universals labelled.
  • Balance** opposite: matched tube and volume. Water is fine.
  • Set 1000 rpm · 5 min · decel 9 · room temp. Close lid.
  • At speed it should be almost silent. Any rumble — stop and rebalance.
  • Wait for the alarm. Remove every tube. Leave the lid open.

MEDIA REFRESHno trypsin, cells stay down

  • Look at the cells. Record appearance and confluence.
  • Aspirate all but a small amount of media, from a corner, never touching the cell floor.
  • Add ~15 mL of the appropriate warmed media.
  • Label the T75 with the refresh date.
  • Return to the incubator, middle of the shelf.

SEEDING PLATESfrom master seed → plate seed

Master seed = resuspended pellet in a universal. Plate seed = that, diluted to the concentration you want to plate at, with wiggle room.
  • 1. Plate seed volume** = (wells × volume per well) + 1 to 2 mL spare.
  • 2. Cells needed** = (cells per well ÷ volume per well) × plate seed volume.
  • 3. Master seed to use** = cells needed ÷ master seed concentration.
  • 4. Top-up media** = plate seed volume − master seed volume.
Worked example3 wells of a 6-well plate, 250k per well
Plate seed volume(3 × 2 mL) = 6 mL → round to 8 mL
Cells needed250 000 ÷ 2 × 8 = 1 000 000
Master seed at 5.5 × 10⁵/mL1 000 000 ÷ 550 000 = 1.8 mL
Top-up media8 − 1.8 = 6.2 mL
  • Write the sums in the lab book. Keep it by the hood.
  • Label the new universal: top = line + PLATE SEED; side = line, x in y.
  • In the hood: add the media first with the pipette boy, then the master seed.
  • Volumes under 1 mL use a micropipette, not a stripette.
  • Resuspend the master seed immediately before drawing from it.
  • Label the plates. Serial dilutions may need extra media in specific wells.
  • Aliquot onto the plate, shoogle on two axes, incubate.
